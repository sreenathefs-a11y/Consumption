What the app does

FM Utility Intelligence shows what changed, where to look, and the next sensible action. It keeps consumption analysis and technical investigation connected without treating every change as a fault.

Daily workflow

  1. Open Home.
  2. Review today’s priorities.
  3. Correct missing or invalid data.
  4. Review consumption changes.
  5. Open only justified alerts.
  6. Complete the recommended action.
  7. Monitor corrective actions.
  8. Confirm savings only after verification.

Monthly workflow

  1. Enter month-end readings.
  2. Verify readings.
  3. Resolve missing data.
  4. Compare with the previous month.
  5. Compare with the same month last year.
  6. Review historical outliers.
  7. Open justified investigations.
  8. Review costs where tariffs exist.
  9. Verify savings.
  10. Generate the monthly report.

How to read statuses

How consumption is classified

Available previous-month, same-month, rolling, historical-range, reading-period, and data-quality evidence is considered. A reduction with missing readings is not a saving. A seasonal increase within a normal July range is not automatically critical.

When an investigation is created

Review an alert first. Start an investigation only when the change remains material and unexplained after basic data validation.

Enter and verify readings

Use Data Entry. Meter reading example: 184562.30. Reading date example: 03 Aug 2026. Verify the meter identity, timestamp, photograph, continuity, CT ratio, and multiplier where applicable.

Add evidence

Upload the file to approved storage, then save its HTTPS link, type, related question, uploader, and verification status in Evidence.

Close a case

Record a confirmed cause, complete the owned corrective action, add post-action verification, and enter the closure approver.

Confirm savings

Compare verified post-action use with a valid baseline. Do not calculate cost when the approved tariff is missing.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Calling every increase a fault.
  • Calling an incomplete reduction a saving.
  • Comparing different utilities or units.
  • Using zero for missing data.
  • Confirming a cause without evidence.
